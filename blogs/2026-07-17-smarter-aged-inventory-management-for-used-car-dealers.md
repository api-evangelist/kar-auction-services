Every dealer knows the impact of aged inventory. As a vehicle spends more time on the lot, carrying costs can increase through floorplan expenses, depreciation, reconditioning investments and the opportunity cost of capital tied up in slower-moving units. But inventory aging begins long before a vehicle reaches a 60, 90 or 120-day milestone.
